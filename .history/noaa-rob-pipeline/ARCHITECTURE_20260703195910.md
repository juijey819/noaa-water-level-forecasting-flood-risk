## NOAA ROB Pipeline — Architecture (summary)

Overview:
- Ingestion: producers/readers collect NOAA data and push to storage or streaming topics.
- Processing: Airflow DAGs orchestrate batch and training jobs located in `dags/`.
- Feature & Model: feature engineering in `src/features/`, model training in `src/models/`, and trained artifacts stored under `models/`.
- Serving & Backend: lightweight `backend/` provides model loading and prediction endpoints.
- Monitoring: Grafana dashboards under `grafana/` and monitoring utilities in `monitoring/`.

Deployment:
- Docker images available for Airflow, PySpark, and monitoring. Use `docker-compose.yml` for local stack.

Notes:
- Keep data, logs, and model artifacts out of VCS (see top-level .gitignore).
- DAGs and infrastructure-as-code are the source of truth for pipeline behavior.
